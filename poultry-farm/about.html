OUR STANDARDS & BIOSECURITY

Poultry Raised with Scientific Precision

We combine data-driven flock management with strict feed integrity. Zero shortcuts, zero unmonitored additives.

01

Controlled Feed Formulation

Custom-milled grain rations balanced for natural growth stages without growth hormones. Every ration batch is formulated on-site with clean maize, roasted soy, essential minerals, and prebiotics for gut health.

02

Bio-Isolation & Pen Sanitation

Restricted farm-gate access, automated disinfection footbaths, and scheduled dry-pen cycles. Our bio-secure perimeter ensures zero unauthorized exposure, reducing mortality and eliminating reliance on prophylactic medications.

03

Humane & Cold-Chain Processing

Same-day slaughter, clean evisceration, and immediate blast-chilling to lock in fresh moisture and eliminate bacterial load. Vacuum-sealed and dispatched without water-weight injection or artificial tenderizers.

The Flock Lifecycle Ledger

Every harvest follows an auditable 42-day precision cycle from brooding to cold dispatch.

Stage 01 · Days 01–14

Day-Old Placement

Monitored brooding chamber temperatures, automated drinker sanitation, and baseline health audits to secure flock vitality.

Stage 02 · Days 15–28

4-Week Growth Check

Structured weight sampling, Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) evaluation, and biometric checks under non-stress ventilation.

Stage 03 · Days 29–41

Pre-Harvest Finishing

Strict fresh water flush, zero chemical supplements, and pen-wide weight categorization targeting 2.3kg – 2.6kg density.

Stage 04 · Day 42

Harvest & Dispatch

Hygienic processing, immediate sub-zero chill, vacuum packaging, and direct fulfillment to pickup points or delivery routes.
